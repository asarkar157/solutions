Develop product positioning and messaging framework.

## Steps

1. Conduct competitive landscape analysis using Crayon/Klue — identify top 3 competitors and their positioning,
2. Run customer interview synthesis to extract top 5 pain points and desired outcomes,
3. Draft value proposition canvas: target persona, pain points addressed, key benefits, proof points,
4. Create messaging hierarchy: tagline (< 10 words), elevator pitch (30 s), one-paragraph positioning statement,
5. Develop competitive differentiation matrix with feature-by-feature comparison,
6. Circulate draft to product, sales, and executive stakeholders for async review (48 h window),
7. Incorporate feedback and publish final messaging doc to Notion/Confluence,
8. Brief content and sales teams in a 30-min enablement session.
